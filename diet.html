Diet and Nutrition

Healthy Eating Guidelines

Here are some general guidelines for healthy eating:

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables
  • Choose whole grains over refined grains
  • Include lean protein sources in your diet
  • Limit added sugars and unhealthy fats
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water

Meal Planning

Plan your meals to ensure you get a balanced diet. Here's an example meal plan:

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with fruit
  • Lunch: Grilled chicken salad
  • Dinner: Baked salmon with vegetables
  • Snacks: Greek yogurt, nuts, or fruit

Recipes

Try these healthy recipes:

  • Avocado Toast
  • Quinoa Salad
  • Stir-Fried Vegetables
