Diet and Nutrition

Nutrition

Nutrition is key to managing kidney disease and your overall health.

Spice up your diet

Giving up salt does not mean giving up flavor. Learn to season your food with herbs and spices.

DASH

The DASH diet has been shown to help decrease blood pressure and lower the risk for heart disease, stroke, cancer, and kidney stones.

SALT & Plant based

Most patients in the early stages of kidney disease need to limit the amount of sodium in their diet. Eating whole grains, nuts, fruits, and vegetables is one of the most important ways to keep kidneys healthy.

Healthy Eating Guidelines

Here are some general guidelines for healthy eating:

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables
  • Choose whole grains over refined grains
  • Include lean protein sources in your diet
  • Limit added sugars and unhealthy fats
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water

Meal Planning

Plan your meals to ensure you get a balanced diet. Here's an example meal plan:

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with fruit
  • Lunch: Grilled chicken salad
  • Dinner: Baked salmon with vegetables
  • Snacks: Greek yogurt, nuts, or fruit

Recipes

Try these healthy recipes:

  • Avocado Toast
  • Quinoa Salad
  • Stir-Fried Vegetables
